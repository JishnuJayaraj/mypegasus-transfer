# Readings

Rows of [the open questions](../open-questions.md) that record how something was built where the
docs didn't decide, were accepted by the owner, and ask nothing more. They moved here with their
numbers, unchanged (Q257: history is separated from unresolved decisions by a row's status and
substance, never by its title). A row that still holds an undecided point, a `verify` fact, a
pilot gate (🔒) or an `open` or `assumed` status is not here but in the open questions; a
`confirmed` one is in [the settled questions](settled-questions.md). Question numbers never
change, so `Qnn` finds its row in exactly one of the three files.

New build history doesn't come here: it belongs in its PR's description, and the rule itself in
the topic's doc ([the agent workflow](../engineering/agent-workflow.md), §3).
