# Answers to open questions — 2026-10-15

Answers from the product owner to `open-questions.md` #1–#39, with the build implications they
gave and a review note where something needs checking. **Assumed** answers are working
assumptions, not confirmed by the owner. The status per question is in `open-questions.md`.

The domain docs and ADRs have **not** been updated from this yet. That is the next docs PR.

## Part 1: original questions

### Q1 — Arbeitsuchendmeldung (ASU)
- Answer: the Arbeitsuchendmeldung (§38 SGB III), a nationwide obligation. It is registered with
  the Agentur responsible for the participant's place of residence. Channels: batch at the info
  event (from a Teilnehmerliste), online via BA eServices, or in person.
- Build: step `asu_registration` with `channel` (`online_self`, `batch_list`, `in_person`),
  `registeredOn` (date), `agenturId`, optional proof upload. The channel default comes from the
  regional template. Export: Teilnehmerliste per Agentur (XLSX/CSV).
- Review notes:
  - There *is* a nationwide form for the TG route: **Kug 200b** (written registration through
    the TG, with the participant's acknowledgement that the TG gets access to their applicant
    profile). It needs AcroForm filling and a wet signature, like TL 407. See `domain/ba-forms.md`
    and Q45.
  - Deadline: as we understand §38(1), the rule is *at the latest 3 months before the end of
    employment*. Only if the person learns of the end less than 3 months in advance is it
    *within 3 days of learning about it*. The answer only mentions the 3 days. Please verify
    before we build reminders on it.

### Q2 — Profiling form
- Answer: one nationwide form, **TL 407 (08/2025)**, AcroForm, handwritten signatures of the
  participant and the profiler. Agenturen differ in submission channel, originals, lead time,
  and whether they reject outdated versions.
- Build: `formCode = TL407`, `formVersion = 08/2025`. Regional config only holds
  `submissionChannel`, `originalRequired`, `leadTimeDays`. Validate dates `TT.MM.JJJJ` before
  rendering.
- Review notes: confirmed by the uploaded copy. The §110/§111 checkbox depends on the project
  type. The form has fixed row limits (Q41). The test copy has an invalid birth date
  (`03.14.1998`), which shows why validation must be in the domain layer.

### Q3 — Retention (assumed)
| Data | Retention | Basis given |
|---|---|---|
| Non-signers | 90 days after the signing deadline | minimisation; grace period for late joiners |
| Unmatched self-registrations | 30 days | minimisation |
| TG payroll and KuG billing documents | 10 years after the end of the calendar year | §147 AO, §257 HGB |
| TG personnel file, DSV, profiling, placement | 3 years after the end of the year of exit | §195 BGB |
| Health and disability details | deleted at exit, unless needed for payroll | Art. 9 GDPR |
| ESF-funded projects | per funding notice, configurable | funding terms |
| Audit log | 3 years; subject references pseudonymised after the subject's data is deleted | accountability |

- Build: retention rules as data (`dataCategory → retentionRule`), a nightly idempotent job
  with the system actor, backups kept ≤ 30 days.
- Review notes:
  - **Conflict** (Q40): TL 407 and Kug 200b contain health and Schwerbehinderung answers but
    are kept 3 years, while health data is deleted at exit. We need either redacted copies or
    an exception.
  - Non-signers who received §110 measures paid by the BA may need records for funding audits
    (Q24, Q40).
  - Retention periods for accounting records were changed recently (Bürokratieentlastungsgesetz
    IV), and payroll accounts (§41 EStG) and Entgeltunterlagen (§28f SGB IV) have their own
    rules. Have the tax advisor/DPO confirm the numbers. Because they are data, a change is a
    config change, not a code change.

### Q4 — Legal basis per purpose (assumed)
| Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Import from the customer (name, contact, Personalnummer) | Art. 6(1)(f), backed by the Transfersozialplan |
| Information, data collection, DSV preparation | Art. 6(1)(b), pre-contractual |
| beE employment, payroll, Transfer-KuG | Art. 6(1)(b) + (c) (SGB III, SGB IV, AO) |
| Profiling and reporting to the Agentur | Art. 6(1)(c) (§110/§111 SGB III) + the signed TL 407 declaration |
| Health / disability | Art. 9(2)(b). Details beyond yes/no only with explicit consent, Art. 9(2)(a) |
| Optional private email/mobile for login | consent, Art. 6(1)(a), revocable |
| Aggregated customer reporting | Art. 6(1)(f), anonymised, with suppression |

- Build: purposes with legal basis. Consent records with purpose, text version, timestamp,
  `revokedAt`. Revoking consent never blocks contract-based processing.
- Review note: if the mobile number is the primary login channel (Q11) but is based on
  revocable consent, revoking it leaves only the assisted mode or a printed access code. That's
  acceptable, but the UX must cover it.

### Q5 — Suppression (assumed)
n < 5 shows "< 5", with secondary suppression so totals can't be used to back-calculate. A
global setting (default 5), implemented in the reporting layer.

### Q6 / Q12 — Hosting (Q6 answered, Q12 assumed)
- k3s on Hetzner Cloud, Nürnberg/Falkenstein only. Postgres via CloudNativePG, encrypted
  backups to Hetzner Object Storage. cert-manager + Let's Encrypt. Prometheus, Grafana, Loki.
  Kubernetes Secrets, SOPS in git.
- Build: Dockerfile (Node 24 LTS), Helm or Kustomize, health/readiness endpoints, JSON logs
  without PII, `/metrics`. Locally: Compose with Postgres 16 + MinIO.
- Review note: the legal entity operating the cluster (AVV) is still open (Q32).

### Q7 — Staff identity (assumed)
Entra ID via OIDC, MFA through Conditional Access. Roles and project assignments are managed
in the app, not from Entra groups. A plain OIDC client (no `IdentityProvider` adapter), a dev
stub login, and server-side sessions in httpOnly SameSite cookies.

### Q8 — Payroll (assumed)
DATEV LODAS via the tax advisor/back office. The MVP has a versioned CSV export: Personalnummer,
name, beE entry/exit, status per day or month, absence types, Aufstockung yes/no. No Steuer-ID,
SV-Nummer or IBAN unless the back-office role explicitly requests them, and that is audited.

### Q9 / Q39 — Languages (Q9 assumed, Q39 answered)
Portal: de + en in the MVP; v1.1 tr, ar (RTL), pl, ru, uk, ro; Leichte Sprache is desirable.
Staff UI: de only, but everything goes through i18n. RTL-ready layout (CSS logical properties).
Legal texts are versioned per language.

### Q10 — Signatures (answered)
Always paper. No e-signature adapter. Track: scan upload, `originalLocation`,
`originalReceivedOn`, `sentToCustomerOn` (and `sentToAgenturOn`, Q21).

### Q11 — Contact channels (assumed)
About 90 % mobile, 70 % private email; 5–10 % need help. Mobile is primary (SMS link or code),
private email secondary, **never imported work emails**. Assisted mode is audited as
`actor = staff`, `onBehalfOf = participant`. Printable one-time access code. Links are
single-use, last 15 minutes, and are rate-limited.
